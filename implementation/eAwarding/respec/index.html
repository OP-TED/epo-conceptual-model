Main Entities

The main entities are those that form the core of the vocabulary. Properties of entities are listed in a tabular form.

Award decision notification

Definition
A formal communication sent by the Buyer to inform Tenderers of the Award Decision.
Subclass of
Document
Properties
For this entity the following properties are defined: concerns award outcome, concerns procedure, has standstill period end date, specifies tenderer.
Property Range Card Definition Usage Feedback

Award outcome

 1..* Relates to Award Outcome.

Procedure

 1 Relates to Procedure.
has standstill period end date Date 0..1 The end date for the mandatory minimum time frame between the communication of a contract award decision and the formal conclusion of the contract, during which unsuccessful Tenderers have the opportunity to challenge the award before it becomes legally binding.
